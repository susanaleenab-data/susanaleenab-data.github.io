Featured · Data Science & Analytics

FuelWise V2 | Smart Vehicle Cost & Energy Planner

Built an interactive Streamlit dashboard to estimate fuel, electricity and travel costs in Indian rupees for petrol, diesel, hybrid, plug-in hybrid and electric vehicles. Includes budget projections, trip logging, CSV-based data auditing and interactive charts.

ML experiment: A training workflow for Linear Regression and Random Forest using actual recorded trips, requiring at least 30 observations per vehicle category. Training on sufficient real-trip data remains pending. Traffic, city driving and AC are logged for future analysis, not used as invented adjustment factors.

Featured · ML Learning Project

FuelWise V1 | Weekly Petrol Planner & ML Lab

Developed a petrol budgeting application with weekly, monthly and annual INR estimates, exploratory dataset auditing and a separate synthetic-data machine-learning demonstration. Tested regression on generated scenarios while keeping formula-based fuel calculations as the budgeting baseline.

Data limitation: The supplied 2,000-row dataset has constant trip distance and formula-derived fuel use. Synthetic model results are educational, not proof of real-world prediction accuracy.

Nourish | Personalized Nutrition & Fitness App

Prototyped with Python and Streamlit, then developed as a Flutter/Dart Android application. Includes food logging with Indian and Kerala foods, protein/carbohydrate/fat/sugar information, profiles, activity visualization and step-data connectivity through Android Health Connect and Google Fit.

Explored personalized hydration, protein and walking estimates, barcode/QR scanning and experimental image-based food recognition. Prediction and image-recognition features are prototypes, not clinically validated AI.
